Carrier: heights of the building rects (the number of lit window rows, since each floor is 10 px tall), grouped into the nine `block-N` groups.

How to read it: Each building is one character. Its height divided by 10 (equivalently, its count of window rows from the 10 px-tall window pattern) is the letter's position in the alphabet, so a one-floor building is the first letter and a 25-floor building is the 25th. Buildings are read left to right in document order, and each `<g id="block-N">` group is one word, with the wider street gaps and lamp posts marking the word breaks. The one building topped by a clock tower is a numeral rather than a letter, and its three floors plus the clock hands pointing to three o'clock give the digit. The flagpole marks the single capitalized letter.
