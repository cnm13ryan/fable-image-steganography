Carrier: two redundant channels — the heights of the building rects in the skyline, and the stroke-dasharray of the stitched picture-frame rect.

How to read it: In the skyline, each building's height divided by 10 gives a letter's position in the alphabet (a=1, b=2, …). Buildings are read left to right in document order; buildings that touch form one word, and the 30px street gaps (also the separate `<g>` blocks) separate words. The thin antenna on the first building marks that letter as a capital, and punctuation is omitted from this channel. In the frame's dasharray, odd-position values are drawn strokes and even-position values are gaps, in Morse timing with unit 3: a 3 is a dot, a 9 is a dash, a 3-gap separates symbols, a 9-gap separates letters, and a 21-gap separates words. The pattern begins at the top-left corner and runs clockwise, and this channel includes the comma.
